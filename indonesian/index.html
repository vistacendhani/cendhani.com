Questions

Before you book

I'm a complete beginner. Can I join?

Yes! Indonesian from Zero starts with foundational everyday language and builds progressively.

What if I have already studied Indonesian?

Take Your Indonesian Further is adapted to the skills, interests and goals you already have.

Are the lessons live?

Yes, these are live, one-to-one online lessons lasting 60 minutes each.

How do I choose the right program?

Start with Indonesian from Zero if you are a complete beginner. Choose Take Your Indonesian Further if you already know some Indonesian. If you have an arrival date for a trip, relocation or longer stay in Indonesia, ask about Indonesia Ready in 20. Unsure? Tell me your goals and I can help you choose.

How is Indonesia Ready in 20 different?

It is for learners with a planned arrival date, whether for travel, relocation or a longer stay. It focuses on practical preparation for your time in Indonesia, including food, shopping, transport, directions, requesting help, daily-life conversations and cultural communication. It includes a needs analysis, personalized preparation roadmap and practical readiness review. The program does not guarantee fluency after 20 lessons.

Are learning materials included?

Lessons come with relevant learning materials tailored to your goals.

How do I schedule and pay?

Choose a program and ask me about availability, including your time zone and preferred lesson times. Use your program’s PayPal.Me link and select “Paying for an item or service.” The recipient name is vista puri cendhani (@vistajones). Once payment is confirmed, contact me with your name and selected program so we can arrange your first lesson personally. Payment does not reserve a calendar slot automatically.

How do rescheduling and package validity work?

Your 10-lesson package is valid for 6 months from your first lesson. Your 20-lesson package is valid for 12 months from your first lesson. Rescheduling is free with at least 24 hours’ notice.

What if we cannot find a suitable lesson time?

After payment, lesson times are arranged personally with me. If we cannot agree on a mutually suitable schedule, you will receive a full refund. You are welcome to ask about availability before paying.

Want to learn Indonesian?

Schedule a free Zoom call to talk about your goals, your current Indonesian, and a lesson schedule that works for you. Send me your time zone and preferred times, and we’ll arrange the call personally.
