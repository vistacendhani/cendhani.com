What do you need Indonesian for?

Lessons are built around your reason for learning, your current level and the situations you want to handle more comfortably.

For work

Practical Indonesian for professionals who communicate with Indonesian colleagues, employees, clients, suppliers or business partners, wherever you are based.

For life in Indonesia

Practical Indonesian for people moving to Indonesia, already living here, or spending significant time here. Complete beginners are welcome.

For travel

Useful Indonesian for holidays and short stays: food, shopping, transport, directions, greetings, simple conversations and getting help when you need it.

Because you want to learn

Learn through conversation, listening, reading, vocabulary, grammar, culture or whatever keeps you interested and moving forward.

Lessons built around you

Lessons can combine speaking, listening, vocabulary, pronunciation, grammar, reading and real-life context. I adapt the material to your level, goals and interests, so you can make steady progress without a one-size-fits-all course.
