Training approach

Practise. Get feedback. Try again.

Employees do not just learn useful expressions. They use them in realistic workplace situations, receive targeted feedback and repeat the task with clearer language.

01

Model & prepare

Explore a realistic example, notice useful language and prepare for the task.

02

Simulate

Use English in a meeting, presentation, discussion, client or writing task.

03

Targeted feedback

The trainer observes and gives specific language and communication feedback.

04

Repeat & improve

Participants try again with clearer language, stronger structure and more confidence.
