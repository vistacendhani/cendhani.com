Structured Business English · A2 sample · Units 1–6
Unit / sessionsCommunication focusLanguage & practical task
1 / 1–3
Introductions		Talk about roles, companies and departments.

Introduce yourself and ask simple professional questions.		Nationalities; to be; a/an with jobs; Wh- questions.

A short work profile, networking role play and introduction email.
2 / 4–6
Work and Leisure		Talk about work routines, schedules and leisure.

Explain a normal working day and a simple work process.		Days, months and dates; present simple; frequency expressions.

Compare routines and describe a typical workday.
3 / 7–9
Problems		Describe familiar problems and ask for clarification.

Make a simple phone enquiry and suggest a solution.		Adjectives; too/enough; simple questions; have; some/any.

Service enquiry role play and short problem-solving email.
Session 10 · Mid-program assessment
4 / 11–13
Travel		Make, change and confirm simple travel arrangements.

Make bookings and handle a familiar travel problem.		Travel details; can/can’t; there is/there are; dates and times.

Booking role play; confirm plans; change a simple itinerary.
5 / 14–16
Food and Entertaining		Talk about food and socialize in familiar work situations.

Make simple suggestions and express polite preferences.		Food vocabulary; some/any; countable and uncountable nouns.

Client lunch role play; choose a suitable restaurant.
6 / 17–19
Buying and Selling		Describe products, prices and straightforward needs.

Compare simple options and agree a basic purchase.		Product vocabulary; past simple; time references; comparisons.

Buyer/seller role play; compare services; agree a simple deal.
Session 20 · Final assessment and practical performance task
